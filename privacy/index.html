Privacy Policy

Blip — Tabata Timer

Effective: November 15, 2026

Blip does not collect any data

Blip is an interval timer for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch.

  • No account, no sign-in.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.
  • Blip makes no network requests. Your workouts and settings are stored only on your iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. Custom workouts, the Mirror with Apple Watch setting and the state of a workout in progress are exchanged between your iPhone and your paired Apple Watch through Apple’s Watch Connectivity and Apple Health. Nothing leaves your devices.

Apple Health

If you allow it:

  • Apple Watch: during a workout Blip reads your heart rate and active energy to show them on screen. When the workout ends, it saves it to Apple Health with an effort score (watchOS 11 or later), which it estimates from your heart rate and your age (read from your date of birth in Health).
  • iPhone and iPad: Blip saves a finished workout to Apple Health when you tap Save to Apple Health, or by itself if you turn on Save Automatically in Blip’s Settings. iPhone can’t measure heart rate, so if you wear Apple Watch, Blip on the watch records the workouts you start on iPhone as described above, and tells your iPhone the active calories and effort score over Apple Health’s connection between your own devices. With Mirror with Apple Watch (Blip’s Settings › Apple Watch, on by default; you can turn it off), iPhone and Apple Watch exchange what they need to run one workout together: which workout it is (its name and its work, rest and round times), when it started, whether it is paused or over, a workout number, whether it was saved, and the active calories and effort score the watch recorded. This travels only between your own iPhone and your own Apple Watch, through Apple Health’s workout mirroring and Apple’s Watch Connectivity. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. A workout you start on Apple Watch can be shown in Blip on your iPhone, where you can pause or end it; the watch saves that workout and the iPhone saves nothing for it.

Health data stays in Apple Health under your control. Blip never sends it anywhere and never uses it for advertising or any purpose other than showing and saving your workouts. You can change Health access at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Health on iPhone or iPad, or in the Health settings on Apple Watch.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Blip schedules local notifications on your iPhone or iPad to announce phase changes while it is locked. They are created on your device and are not sent through any server.

Children

Blip does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
