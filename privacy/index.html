Privacy Policy

Blip — Tabata Timer

Effective: October 22, 2026

Blip does not collect any data

Blip is an interval timer for iPhone and Apple Watch.

  • No account, no sign-in.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.
  • Blip makes no network requests. Your workouts and settings are stored only on your iPhone and Apple Watch. Custom workouts are copied from your iPhone to your paired Apple Watch through Apple’s Watch Connectivity.

Apple Health

If you allow it:

  • Apple Watch: during a workout Blip reads your heart rate and active energy to show them on screen, and saves the finished workout to Apple Health.
  • iPhone: Blip saves a finished workout to Apple Health when you tap Save to Health.

Health data stays in Apple Health under your control. Blip never sends it anywhere and never uses it for advertising or any purpose other than showing and saving your workouts. You can change Health access at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Health on iPhone, or in the Health settings on Apple Watch.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Blip schedules local notifications on your iPhone to announce phase changes while the phone is locked. They are created on your device and are not sent through any server.

Children

Blip does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
