Blip

Tabata & HIIT timer for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch

Download on the App Store

Quick answers

How do I start a workout?

Tap a workout. A 10-second “Get ready” countdown starts, then the screen turns green for work and red for rest. Tap Pause to take a break, or the X button to stop.

How do I create my own workout?

On iPhone or iPad tap New, set work, rest, rounds and get-ready time, then tap Save. Long-press your workout to edit or delete it. Workouts you make on iPhone also appear on your Apple Watch automatically.

Will I hear the cues if I lock my iPhone or iPad?

Yes, if you allow notifications: Blip keeps announcing phase changes on the lock screen with the matching sound. While Blip is open, the screen stays on. If you declined notifications, turn them on in Settings › Notifications › Blip.

Can I train without sound?

Yes. Blip’s cues play even in silent mode, so open Settings in Blip (gear icon) and turn Sound off. Haptics keep working. Other music only dips for a moment while a cue plays.

Does Blip work on Apple Watch without my iPhone?

Yes. Start any workout on the watch. It keeps running with your wrist down, shows your heart rate and calories, and saves the workout to Apple Health. The Quick Start complication starts your last workout from the watch face.

Why does Blip open on my Apple Watch when I start a workout on iPhone?

iPhone can’t measure heart rate. With Blip on your Apple Watch, the watch joins the workout you started on iPhone and records it: heart rate, active calories and effort. It also taps your wrist at each interval. You can pause and end it on either device. When it ends, save it on either one. To leave the watch out, turn off Mirror with Apple Watch in Blip’s Settings.

What is Mirror with Apple Watch, and how does it work?

With Mirror with Apple Watch, a workout runs on your iPhone and your Apple Watch at once, whichever one you start on. Pause, Resume and End work on both. When the watch takes part, it times the workout and records your heart rate, active calories and, on watchOS 11 or later, an effort estimate, and exactly one workout goes to Apple Health. Starting on one device never takes over the other’s screen: if Blip is already open on your iPhone while a workout runs on the watch, a bar “Workout on Apple Watch · View” appears at the bottom of Home. You can pause on your wrist with the iPhone locked in a pocket. This needs an iPhone with an Apple Watch that has Blip installed.

How do I turn Mirror with Apple Watch off?

On iPhone open Blip’s Settings (gear icon) › Apple Health and turn off Mirror with Apple Watch. Each workout then stays on the device where you start it: the watch is left out of iPhone workouts, and a workout on the watch doesn’t show on the iPhone. It takes effect from the next workout. The switch isn’t shown on iPad, which doesn’t pair with Apple Watch.

What happens if I have no Apple Watch, or it is out of range?

Without an Apple Watch, Blip works on your iPhone or iPad as before; nothing else is needed. If the watch is out of range or can’t be reached when you start on iPhone, the workout simply runs on the iPhone and you save it from there. If the connection drops during a workout, the watch keeps timing and recording and the iPhone keeps counting; the watch symbol on the iPhone is struck through until they find each other again.

I’m recording a workout with another app on my Apple Watch. Can I use Blip as a timer on iPhone?

If you’re recording in another workout app on the watch, turn Mirror with Apple Watch off first, because Apple Watch runs one workout at a time. Otherwise Blip’s workout on the watch can end the other app’s.

How do I do another round?

Tap Repeat on the completion screen to run the same workout again. On Apple Watch, tap the same workout in the list.

Where are my workouts saved?

Workouts started on Apple Watch are saved to Apple Health automatically, with an effort score estimated from your heart rate (watchOS 11 or later). On iPhone and iPad, tap Save to Apple Health on the completion screen, or turn on Save Automatically in Blip’s Settings. If you wear Apple Watch while a workout runs on iPhone, the watch records it, so it’s saved with heart rate, active calories and effort.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. Blip has no account, no ads and no in-app purchases, and it works fully offline.

What do I need to run Blip?

An iPhone with iOS 17 or later, or an iPad with iPadOS 17 or later. The Apple Watch app works with iPhone, needs watchOS 10 or later (Apple Watch Series 4, SE or newer) and is optional; the effort score in Apple Health needs watchOS 11 or later.
