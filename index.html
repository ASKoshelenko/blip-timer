Blip

Tabata & HIIT timer for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch

Download on the App Store

Quick answers

How do I start a workout?

Tap a workout. A 10-second “Get ready” countdown starts, then the screen turns green for work and red for rest. Tap Pause to take a break, or the X button to stop. A workout you stop with X on iPhone isn’t saved.

How do I create my own workout?

On iPhone or iPad tap New, set work, rest, rounds and get-ready time, then tap Save. Set rest to 0 for work after work, like an EMOM. A new workout is added at the end, next to New. Long-press one of your own to edit or delete it; Delete asks first. To put any workout in a different place, built-in ones too, touch and hold its card, then drag it. Workouts you make on iPhone also appear on your Apple Watch automatically.

Will I hear the cues if I lock my iPhone or iPad?

Yes, if you allow notifications: Blip keeps announcing phase changes on the lock screen with the matching sound. While the timer runs in Blip, the screen stays on. If you declined notifications, Blip’s Settings shows “Lock-screen cues are off” with an Open Settings button; you can also turn them on in Settings › Notifications › Blip. iOS lets an app keep about 60 announcements at a time, so in a workout of more than 30 rounds (more than 50 with rest 0) the lock screen announces every work start and the end, but not the rests of the later rounds.

Can I train without sound?

Yes. Blip’s cues play even in silent mode, so open Settings in Blip (gear icon) and turn Sound off. Haptics keep working. Other music only dips for a moment while a cue plays.

Does Blip work on Apple Watch without my iPhone?

Yes. Start any workout on the watch. It keeps running with your wrist down, shows your heart rate and calories, and saves the workout to Apple Health. The Quick Start complication starts your last workout from the watch face.

Why does Blip open on my Apple Watch when I start a workout on iPhone?

iPhone can’t measure heart rate. With Blip on your Apple Watch, the watch joins the workout you started on iPhone and records it: heart rate, active calories and effort. It also taps your wrist at each interval. You can pause and end it on either device. When it ends, save it on either one. To leave the watch out, turn off Mirror with Apple Watch in Blip’s Settings.

What is Mirror with Apple Watch, and how does it work?

With Mirror with Apple Watch, a workout runs on your iPhone and your Apple Watch at once, whichever one you start on. Pause, Resume and End work on both. When the watch takes part, it times the workout and records your heart rate, active calories and, on watchOS 11 or later, an effort estimate, and exactly one workout goes to Apple Health. Starting on one device never takes over the other’s screen: if Blip is already open on your iPhone while a workout runs on the watch, a bar “Workout on Apple Watch · View” appears at the bottom of Home. You can pause on your wrist with the iPhone locked in a pocket. This needs an iPhone with an Apple Watch that has Blip installed.

How do I turn Mirror with Apple Watch off?

On iPhone open Blip’s Settings (gear icon) › Apple Watch and turn off Mirror with Apple Watch. Each workout then stays on the device where you start it: the watch is left out of iPhone workouts, and a workout on the watch doesn’t show on the iPhone. It takes effect from the next workout. The Apple Watch section isn’t shown on iPad, which doesn’t pair with Apple Watch, or on an iPhone without a paired watch.

What happens if I have no Apple Watch, or it is out of range?

Without an Apple Watch, Blip works on your iPhone or iPad as before; nothing else is needed. If the watch is out of range or can’t be reached when you start on iPhone, the workout simply runs on the iPhone and you save it from there. If the connection drops during a workout, the watch keeps timing and recording and the iPhone keeps counting; the watch symbol on the iPhone is struck through until they find each other again.

I’m recording a workout with another app on my Apple Watch. Can I use Blip as a timer on iPhone?

If you’re recording in another workout app on the watch, turn Mirror with Apple Watch off first, because Apple Watch runs one workout at a time. Otherwise Blip’s workout on the watch can end the other app’s.

Why did my iPhone screen go dark while I was paused?

A paused workout keeps the screen on for five minutes, so Resume stays one tap away. After that the iPhone may dim and lock as usual; unlock it and tap Resume. No cues are given while paused. When Apple Watch times the workout, a pause of 30 minutes ends it.

Blip was closed during my workout. What now?

If iOS closes Blip in the background while a workout runs on iPhone alone, the lock-screen cues keep coming, and the workout is back when you open Blip: running at the right second, or paused (for up to 30 minutes), or on its completion screen if it ended meanwhile (for up to 24 hours). That screen always shows the Save button, even with Save Automatically on: nothing is saved until you tap it. If you swipe Blip away in the app switcher while it is still open, the workout and its cues end. If you swipe it away after iOS has already suspended it, Blip can’t tell that from iOS closing it, and the workout comes back. A workout that Apple Watch takes part in is handled by the watch.

Why did Save Automatically turn on?

The first time you tap Save to Apple Health and it works, Blip turns on Save Automatically and tells you once on that screen, unless you have ever changed the switch yourself. It applies from the next workout. Turn it off in Blip’s Settings and it stays off.

How do I do another round?

Tap Repeat on the completion screen to run the same workout again. On Apple Watch, tap the same workout in the list.

Where are my workouts saved?

Workouts started on Apple Watch are saved to Apple Health automatically, with an effort score estimated from your heart rate (watchOS 11 or later). On iPhone and iPad, tap Save to Apple Health on the completion screen, or turn on Save Automatically in Blip’s Settings. The time on the completion screen is your active time, without pauses, as Apple Health counts it. If you wear Apple Watch while a workout runs on iPhone, the watch records it, so it’s saved with heart rate, active calories and effort.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. Blip has no account, no ads and no in-app purchases, and it works fully offline.

What do I need to run Blip?

An iPhone with iOS 17 or later, or an iPad with iPadOS 17 or later. The Apple Watch app works with iPhone, needs watchOS 10 or later (Apple Watch Series 4, SE or newer) and is optional; the effort score in Apple Health needs watchOS 11 or later.
